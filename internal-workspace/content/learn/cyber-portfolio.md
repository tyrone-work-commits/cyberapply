# Build a portfolio that explains your thinking

A cybersecurity portfolio can demonstrate how you approach a problem. The strongest artifact is not necessarily the largest project; it is one a reviewer can understand, reproduce where appropriate and discuss with you.

## Include the reasoning

Describe the question, authorized environment, method, result and limitations. Explain why you chose the tools and how you checked your conclusion. Use fictional or approved data when a real example would reveal confidential information.

- A detection lab can include the hypothesis, sample events and tuning decisions.
- A risk example can describe the assets, assumptions and treatment choices.
- A code project can include tests, setup instructions and known limitations.

Keep credentials, tokens, private addresses and employer data out of public repositories and screenshots. Link only work you may share. Treat a lab as a lab in your CV, and make the connection to the vacancy through the actual tasks you completed.
