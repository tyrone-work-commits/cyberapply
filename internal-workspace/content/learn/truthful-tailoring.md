# Tailor a CV without changing your history

Tailoring means making relevant evidence easy to find. It does not mean adding every requirement from a job description. Start by separating skills you can demonstrate from skills you are still learning.

## Work through the gaps

For each missing term, ask whether your existing experience supports it. If yes, clarify the wording or add the specific real example. If no, leave it as a development goal. A related tool is not automatically equivalent to the employer’s requested tool.

Move the most relevant work toward the beginning of a section. Keep job titles, employers, qualifications and dates accurate. A concise summary and a few stronger bullets usually help more than keyword repetition.

Use Compare before exporting an optimized version. Review both readability and factual consistency. Your chosen application CV changes only when you explicitly choose the new file; editing an optimizer session should not silently replace the original.
