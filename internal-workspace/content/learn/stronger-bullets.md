# Turn responsibilities into clear work examples

“Responsible for security” tells a recruiter little. A useful bullet explains what you did, how you did it and the result you can actually establish. You do not need to invent a percentage to make a bullet specific.

## Start with an action

Use a verb that matches your contribution: investigated, reviewed, documented, configured or tested. Name a tool only if you used it. Distinguish your own actions from the team’s work.

- What problem or task were you given?
- What evidence or method did you use?
- What changed, or what did you learn?

An illustrative pattern is: “Reviewed [type of events] in [tool], documented [findings] and escalated cases meeting [criteria].” Replace the brackets with real details. When no measurable outcome exists, describe the scope or verified deliverable rather than creating a number. Keep confidential employer information out of the example.
