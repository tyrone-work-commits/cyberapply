# Prepare a few real STAR stories

Behavioural questions ask about how you handled a situation. A useful answer makes the context understandable and your own contribution visible. STAR is a structure for organizing evidence, not a reason to manufacture an achievement.

## Four short parts

- Situation: explain the relevant context.
- Task: describe what you needed to achieve.
- Action: explain what you actually did and why.
- Result: state the real outcome and what you learned.

Prepare a few examples covering collaboration, a difficult decision, learning and a mistake. Keep enough detail to answer a follow-up question. Do not turn a team outcome into an individual accomplishment.

Use practice feedback to notice missing context or vague actions. A basic coaching score cannot determine your employability. For a hypothetical answer, say what you would do rather than presenting it as a past event. Retake a practice question after editing the structure, while keeping the facts unchanged.
