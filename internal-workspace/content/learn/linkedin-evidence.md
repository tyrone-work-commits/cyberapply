# Make your LinkedIn profile consistent with your CV

Your profile can explain your professional direction in a more conversational way than a CV, but the underlying facts should agree. Check job dates, titles, qualifications and contact links before polishing a headline.

## Compare several target roles

Use three different descriptions to identify recurring skills. Prioritize terms you can support with actual work or clearly labelled projects. A skill’s frequency in job postings does not prove that you possess it.

Write a headline that communicates your focus. Use About to connect your background with the contribution you want to make. Add public work to Featured only when you have permission to share it.

CyberApply reads only the profile content you provide. It does not sign into or change LinkedIn. Copy a suggestion only after checking every claim. Profile scores describe keyword alignment in the supplied text, not visibility in LinkedIn’s search or a recruiter’s decision.
