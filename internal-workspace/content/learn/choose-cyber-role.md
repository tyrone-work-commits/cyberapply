# Choose a cybersecurity role by its work

Cybersecurity titles overlap. Read the responsibilities before deciding whether a vacancy fits. A SOC analyst may spend the day validating alerts, while a GRC analyst may spend it documenting controls and discussing risk. Neither title alone tells you the tools, seniority or eligibility requirements.

## Compare three real descriptions

Write down the recurring tasks, required skills and expected outcomes. Separate requirements from preferences. Note location, work authorization and experience requirements separately; a strong keyword score does not establish eligibility.

- Choose a role family you can explain with evidence from work, study or clearly labelled labs.
- Identify one practical gap you can work on.
- Save relevant vacancies and record why they fit.

Use Explore for discovery and the tracker for your shortlist. Change direction when the work itself interests you, rather than because a title sounds impressive. Keep your preferred role separate from claims about experience you already possess.
