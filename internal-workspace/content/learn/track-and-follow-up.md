# Track applications without confusing a request with a receipt

A useful tracker distinguishes what you intended to do from what the employer confirmed. Choosing Apply, seeing a form filled and receiving a confirmation are different events. Keep the source URL and the materials used with each record.

## Keep the next action clear

Record interview times, contacts, notes and follow-up tasks. When you change a personal tracking stage, remember that this does not create employer evidence. A user-reported application made elsewhere is also a separate kind of record.

If delivery is uncertain, check the employer website and relevant email before trying again. Repeating Submit can create duplicates. If an acknowledgment is delayed, retain the recorded attempt and ask the employer to verify receipt rather than assuming success or failure.

After an interview, draft a short thank-you note mentioning something you actually discussed. Review it before sending. Use reminders to organize your actions, not as proof that an application or message has already been delivered.
