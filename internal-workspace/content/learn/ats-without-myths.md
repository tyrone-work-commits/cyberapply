# Understand ATS feedback without chasing a magic score

A CV scanner compares text and structure with a job description. It cannot know an employer’s private screening rules or guarantee an interview. A missing keyword may mean you described a real skill differently, or it may reveal a genuine gap.

## Use the evidence

Open the report’s keyword rows and check the surrounding text. Add an exact term only when it accurately describes your background. Repeating a keyword in unrelated sections does not make you more qualified.

- Use standard headings and readable text.
- Keep contact information in the document body.
- Verify the employer’s parsed fields after uploading.
- Retain the original CV and compare any edited version.

CyberApply explains its own match score. Formatting advice and role eligibility are separate checks. Prioritize a clear, truthful document a recruiter can follow instead of treating a numerical score as an offer prediction.
