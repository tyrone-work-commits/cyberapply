# Show the bridge from your previous work

A career change does not require pretending your previous role was cybersecurity. Identify the work that transfers: troubleshooting, software testing, documentation, customer communication, automation or handling sensitive information.

## Connect tasks, not labels

Choose a real example, describe the method you used and explain why it matters for the target role. Be precise about where your cybersecurity experience begins. Three years of total professional experience is not automatically three years in security.

Use a Projects section for relevant labs or personal work. Label the context and show what you tested, found or learned. A small, well-explained project is more useful than an unsupported list of advanced tools.

In a cover letter, acknowledge the transition and connect a verified strength to an advertised responsibility. Keep learning goals separate from existing qualifications. Select roles whose actual requirements fit your current evidence and development plan rather than relying on a title or match score alone.
