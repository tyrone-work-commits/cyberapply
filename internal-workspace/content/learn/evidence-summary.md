# Write a summary you can support

A useful summary tells the reader your focus, relevant strengths and the kind of contribution you can substantiate. It does not need to call you an expert or list every security tool in the vacancy.

## Build from three facts

Choose your intended role, a relevant area of actual experience and one concrete type of work. If you are changing careers, describe transferable work accurately. If your experience is from a lab, say so rather than presenting it as paid employment.

Replace this template with true details: “Candidate targeting [role], with documented experience in [actual area]. Used [verified tools] to [real task].” Remove any clause you cannot support.

Read the finished summary against the rest of your CV. Dates, tools and seniority should agree. In the optimizer, accept wording only after checking the underlying evidence. Save it as a new version so you can compare it with the original.
