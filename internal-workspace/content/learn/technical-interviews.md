# Explain your technical process

In a technical interview, a clear reasoning process can matter as much as the final answer. Start by clarifying the environment, available evidence and constraints. State assumptions instead of silently filling gaps.

## Make the sequence visible

For an investigation, explain how you would validate the alert, assess impact, preserve evidence and decide on escalation or containment. For a design question, identify the assets, trust boundaries, likely threats and how you would test a mitigation.

Describe tools as part of a method, not as a list of product names. Explain what information each tool would provide and what you would do if the result contradicted your first hypothesis.

When you do not know something, explain how you would find out safely. Separate practical experience from a proposed approach. Practice beforehand in CyberApply, then complete the real interview according to the interviewer’s rules. The practice tool is not a live interview assistant.
