# CyberApply internal privacy notice

This notice describes the current internal workspace. Covenda should review its policy and support details before broader use.

## Your records

CyberApply stores your profile, chosen CV files, career-tool documents, job choices, application requests and their history under your account. Other workspace members do not automatically receive your private records. Site editing and workspace membership are managed separately.

## Employer applications

Job discovery reads public vacancy sources. A chosen application may send your reviewed CV, profile answers and materials to the employer through an authorized browser runner. New sensitive optional profile additions are not automatically included. An uncertain outcome remains unverified until evidence is available.

## Connected services

The application inbox uses separately authorized read-only Gmail access and checks that the mailbox matches your sign-in email. CyberApply does not change your mail. A configured writing or address provider can receive the content of an explicit request; these optional providers are off by default. Address lookup also requires your explicit query consent. Browser speech recognition is separate and may use the browser vendor’s service.

## Recordings

Application recordings stay with the application evidence until removed. Interview-practice recordings expire for playback after 30 days; physical deletion runs when your practice tools are next active. Microphone, camera and geolocation start only through your explicit action and browser permission. No device permission is required for text practice or manual address entry.

## Your controls

Use Settings to export your records, reset this app’s local cache, sign out or request typed-confirmation deletion of your CyberApply data and private files. Deletion does not withdraw an employer application or delete your ChatGPT account. Removing local cache does not remove server records. Support drafts and feedback remain private until an actual support-sharing workflow is configured.
