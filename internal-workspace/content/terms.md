# CyberApply internal usage terms

Internal working notice for Covenda review. This page does not introduce a payment plan or replace your employer’s policies.

## Use accurate information

Use your own authorized CV and accurate saved profile. Review generated suggestions before accepting them. Missing experience, qualifications, salary, work authorization or consent must not be invented. A keyword score is guidance and cannot guarantee hiring outcomes.

## Applications and evidence

Choosing Apply authorizes only the workflow described in your application settings. Employer requirements and verification still apply. Do not bypass challenges, misrepresent authorship or retry a possibly delivered application without checking its outcome. Applied requires employer evidence; a personal tracker stage is not confirmation.

## Private career tools

Practice interviews are for preparation and must not be used as a live interview helper. Use only files and reference material you may share. Do not upload passwords, verification codes or API keys into career documents or support messages.

## Optional services

Email/push delivery, paid writing providers, address services and commercial features require separate setup and authorization. No payment processor is enabled by this upgrade. Ask the workspace owner about access, costs, retention and support responsibilities before broader use.
